🛒 Orders

Central hub for every pending customer order, imported from Tally.

Importing orders

1

Export your pending sales order report from Tally as .xlsx.

2

Go to Orders → Setup and click Import Orders, choose the file.

3

If any customer isn't in the route/transporter master list yet, you'll be asked to fill in their route and transporter before the import finishes.

4

Orders already dispatched are never reset. Partially dispatched orders keep their dispatched quantity — only the remaining balance updates from Tally.

5

Any order no longer present in the new file (i.e. closed in Tally) is automatically marked Pre-closed.

Transport / customer master list

Under Orders → Setup, you can manually add or edit a customer's route, transporter, and whether they're Direct or via a named transporter. You can also bulk-import this list from a spreadsheet.

When importing the transport master list, the header row is now detected automatically by its content (Customer/Particulars/Party, Transport/Transporter, Route/Type/Area) — it no longer needs to be in a fixed position.

📍 Site Delivery

Sometimes an order is billed to one customer but physically delivered somewhere else — a project site, or a location on a completely different route. Site Delivery lets you override the route/transporter for one specific order without changing the customer's normal account details.

How to flag an order

1

In Orders → Pending, expand the customer's card to see their individual orders.

2

Click 📍 Mark Site Delivery next to the order's voucher number.

3

Search and select (or type a new) Actual Delivery Route/Area and Transport. New entries are automatically added to the master route/transporter list.

4

Save. The order now shows a purple 📍 Site Delivery → [route] / [transport] badge and moves under the new route everywhere in the system — Dispatch Today, Direct/Transport Prep, sequencing, printed lists, and reports.

Only that one order is affected. The customer's other orders and their default account route remain untouched. Click ✕ Remove Override at any time to revert it.
This survives Tally re-import — the flag, route, and transporter stay locked in for that order even after you re-upload the full order file, as long as the order is still pending.

Who can flag a site delivery: Admin, Issuer, HO.

🚚 Dispatch Today

Shows everything dispatched today, split into two sections:

  • 🏢 TRANSPORTER ORDERS — sequenced by your uploaded transporter sequence
  • 🚚 DIRECT ORDERS — sequenced by your uploaded per-route direct delivery sequence

Each section numbers independently, so the sequence number matches what your loading team sees on the ground. Print the dispatch list from here — it keeps the same two-section layout on paper.

📦 Direct Prep & Transport Prep

Both tabs show a Preparation Summary panel alongside the order list, with three views:

ViewShows
👥 CustomerSame breakdown as the order list, with sequence number, cartons/loose count
📦 ProductTotal quantity needed per product across all customers, with volume and per-customer split
🗺 Route / 🏢 TransporterGrouped by route or transporter — most useful when viewing "All"

Direct Prep route tabs

Selecting a route (e.g. Bombay) shows customers on that route and any customer explicitly added to that route's uploaded sequence file, even if their registered route is different — this is separate from Site Delivery, and is meant for cases where a delivery sequence sheet intentionally groups customers across routes for a single trip. Look for the amber ↳ added to [route] sequence tag.

If a customer appears in more than one route's sequence file, they'll show up under every matching route tab.

🔢 Delivery Sequences

Upload a sequence file per transporter, and a separate one per direct-delivery route, from Orders → Setup. Format: two columns — a sequence number and a name — in either column order (the system auto-detects which is which).

After uploading, "Missing from Sequence" downloads a clean list of only the transporters (or customers, for Direct) that aren't yet in your file — nothing else clutters the download.

📊 Reports

Under Orders → Reports, filter by:

  • Transport — pick All, a specific named transporter, or 🚚 Direct — all routes
  • Direct Route — appears only when Direct is selected, narrows to one route (e.g. Bombay)

Print an individual delivery list per transporter or per direct route this way, instead of one mixed list.

⚠️ Complaints

Logging a complaint

Salesmen logging their own complaint skip the "Where was this received" question entirely — it's assumed to be them. Admin/QC/Issuer/HO still choose between Received by Salesman or Received at HO.

For HO-received complaints, you can leave Assign to Salesman as "— Assign later —" and come back to it afterward.

Assigning (or re-assigning) a salesman later

Open any complaint with no salesman assigned yet — Admin/HO will see an inline Assign panel right in the complaint's detail view. Picking a salesman here notifies them immediately and works exactly like assigning at creation time.

Field visit

Once assigned, the salesman sees a 📍 Add Field Visit Details button with a Visited Customer? dropdown (Yes / Not yet / Scheduled), visit notes, and photo upload.

List view

Each complaint is a single compact row: Customer · Product · Date · Assigned Salesman · Status. Toggle 👥 By Customer to group repeat complaints — a red badge flags any customer with more than one complaint on file.

"Open" filter means "not yet Resolved or Closed" — it includes every in-progress stage, not just the literal "Open" status.

Visibility

A salesman sees complaints they logged and any complaint assigned to them by someone else — but never another salesman's complaints.

💧 Leakage

Found under the ⚠️ Complaints tab, as a sub-tab next to Complaints (users with only Leakage access go straight there).

Reporting a leak

Customer, product, batch no, invoice no, date, quantity leaked (with unit), description, and photo/video evidence. On-screen reminder: get a photo of the batch number, the leak itself, and the invoice if possible.

The decision flow

1. Admin Response — Admin reviews and picks one:
  • Visit Customer — salesman gets a notes form (with photo upload) to fill in after visiting
  • Issue Credit Note — HO gets notified to issue it
  • Reject — salesman gets a notes form to acknowledge
  • Close (No Credit Note) — closes it directly, no further action needed
Admin can also attach more photos here if the original upload wasn't enough, and can change the decision anytime until the case is finalized.
2. Salesman follow-up (Visit Customer / Reject only) — adds notes (and photos, for a visit). Submitting auto-closes the case.
3. HO issues the credit note (Credit Note Approved only) — enters CN number, amount, date, and clicks ✓ Mark Credit Note Issued. The system silently timestamps this, so the workspace shows "Took X days from approval to credit note being issued" — a turnaround-time record for HO.

Who sees what

RoleCan view
Admin, Issuer, HOEvery leakage report
Sales, Store, QCOnly reports they personally added

List view supports the same 📋 List / 👥 By Customer toggle as Complaints, with a teal badge for customers with repeat leakage issues.

🧪 R&D & Trials

Total Trial Batch Size

When logging or editing a trial, set an exact Total Trial Batch Size (quantity + unit). It prints in bold on the individual Trial Sheet and as its own row on the Trial Comparison Sheet, so the team makes exactly that much — not more, not less.

Test report results on the comparison sheet

If a trial already has a completed Test Report, the Trial Comparison print pulls in the actual results (with a Pass/Fail/Marginal tag) automatically. Trials without a report yet print a blank line for the team to fill by hand.

BOM — Download All (ZIP)

Bundles every product's BOM into one ZIP, sorted into folders by product Category (Decorative/, Industrial/, Automotive/…), each file containing the Current version plus all Archived versions as separate sheets.

📋 BOM

Each product's Bill of Materials, with full version history. Archived versions are kept as separate sheets when exporting, so nothing is lost when a formulation changes.

🏪 Stock

Raw material stock levels, daily consumption logging, and import/export tools. Store role has view + consume access by default.
